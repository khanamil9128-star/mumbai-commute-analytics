Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

Visual analysis generated using Matplotlib and Seaborn exploring key commute dynamics

Speed & Distance Insight

Local trains offer the lowest minutes per km over long journeys (>15 km). For shorter trips (<5 km), wait times and access walks make autos competitive despite traffic.

Cost Efficiency Insight

Mumbai Local train is 85% cheaper than Auto Rickshaws and 60% cheaper than Metro. It remains the most economical public transport choice in the city.

Congestion & Peak Hours

Peak hours increase Auto travel time by ~48% due to street bottlenecks, whereas Metro travel time is barely affected (+7%), though passenger crowding increases.
