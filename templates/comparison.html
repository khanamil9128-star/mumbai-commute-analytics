Transport Mode Comparison

Detailed head-to-head empirical trade-off analysis: Metro vs Local Train vs Auto Rickshaw

Metro

Modern Transit

High reliability, punctual schedules, fully air-conditioned, moderate fares, immune to street traffic jams.

  • Typical Distance: 3.0 – 26.0 km
  • Average Fare: ₹20 – ₹60
  • Delays: Minimal (1–3 min)
  • Comfort Score: 4.5 / 5.0 (High)
  • Crowd Stress: Moderate to High in peak
Best For: Commuters prioritizing punctuality, comfort, and AC travel over medium suburban distances.

Mumbai Local

Mumbai Lifeline

Unrivalled high passenger capacity, hyper-affordable fares, rapid long-distance coverage across Western, Central & Harbour corridors.

  • Typical Distance: 8.0 – 42.0+ km
  • Average Fare: ₹10 – ₹25
  • Delays: Moderate (4–12 min)
  • Comfort Score: 2.5 / 5.0 (Basic)
  • Crowd Stress: Extreme during peak rush
Best For: Long-distance daily office commutes needing minimum cost and maximum speed.

Auto Rickshaw

First/Last Mile

Personalized point-to-point mobility in suburbs, private seating, zero crowd congestion, but vulnerable to road traffic bottlenecks and higher fares.

  • Typical Distance: 1.5 – 14.0 km
  • Average Fare: ₹40 – ₹250
  • Delays: High in peak street traffic
  • Comfort Score: 3.2 / 5.0 (Moderate)
  • Crowd Stress: Low (Private Cabin)
Best For: Short-hop trips, luggage transfers, or reaching railway/metro stations directly.
